Delete your Kull account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or by email. Kull is an Android app for keeping your Salah Streak.

Last updated 6 November 2026

In the app

Open Kull, go to Settings → Account → Delete account, and confirm. The account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email support@theepochguy.in from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Kull account”. We delete the account and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

Everything our server holds about you: your account, email address, password hash, handle, display name, the settings saved with your account (gender and whether exempt days are on), and any record of failed sign-in attempts with your handle.

Your prayer logs, streak, notes, tasbih counts and exempt days were never on our server. They live only on your phone. Clear the app's storage or uninstall Kull to remove them, and delete any backup file you saved yourself.

What is kept

Nothing is kept on purpose. Our hosting provider's routine backups may hold a copy for a short time until they are replaced.
