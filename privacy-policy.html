Kull — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

Kull helps you keep your Salah Streak. You need an account to sign in, but your prayer records and prayer times stay on your phone and work without a network, and its privacy rules follow from that. The same text is in the app itself, at Settings → About → Privacy policy.

What never leaves your phone

Your prayer logs, your streak, your repairs, your notes, your tasbih counts and the days you mark as exempt are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded, never backed up to us, and never shared. Days you mark as exempt are held in a separate, encrypted database on the phone. They are left out of Android's automatic backups. Settings can save a password-protected backup file of your prayer log, streak, qada and progress, if you choose to. Without that file, uninstalling the app removes this data.

Your account

You need an account to use Kull. Our backend provider Supabase stores your email address, your handle, your display name and, visible only to you, your gender and whether exempt days are switched on — so your settings return when you sign in on a new phone. Nothing about your prayers is stored there. Your password is stored only as a cryptographic hash.

Location

The app uses your location to calculate prayer times and the direction of the qibla, and that calculation happens on the phone. You can enter a city by hand instead and never grant location access. When you search for a nearby masjid, your approximate coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service to fetch nearby mosques; that request is not tied to your account. To find your location and turn it into a place name, the phone uses Google Play services and Android's geocoding service, usually run by Google, which may receive your coordinates or the city you type. The app remembers only your latest position, on this phone, so prayer times are ready when you open it. It is not uploaded and is left out of backup files. We do not keep location history.

No tracking, no ads

There is no analytics, no advertising, no third-party tracking and no crash reporting in this app. We do not sell or share personal data, and nothing here is used for advertising.

Deleting your data

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently deletes your account, your email and your handle from the server.

Without the app: email support@theepochguy.in from the address you signed up with, asking for deletion, and the account is deleted.

Data held on your phone is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Step by step, including what is deleted: Delete your Kull account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or a deletion request: support@theepochguy.in
